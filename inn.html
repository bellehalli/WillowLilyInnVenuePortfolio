How Inn access works

Know before you book.

Sunday rental

Daytime Inn access is included with the Sunday wedding rental. Overnight accommodation is not part of the Sunday package.

One Day rental

Inn access begins at 9 AM on your Friday or Saturday wedding day. Confirm overnight specifics during booking.

Full Weekend

Friday daytime and overnight Inn stay, Saturday all-day access and overnight Inn stay, then Sunday checkout at 11 AM.

Pets

Pets can participate in the ceremony and reception. They are not generally permitted loose in the Inn, though a crated pet may be accommodated in a designated area.
