Catering

Dinner without a commercial kitchen

Ask about mobile preparation, temperature control, power, water, waste and service staffing. Share the estate access window before approving your timeline.

Photography

One estate, several kinds of light

Ask to see complete galleries in weather similar to your season. Plan portrait routes between your ceremony, water, bridge and barn.

Coordination

Someone who owns the transitions

Confirm who cues the ceremony, handles the rain decision, coordinates vendors and closes the property at the end of the night.

Transport

A comfortable way home

Ask about wheelchair-accessible vehicles, return journeys, final departures and safe boarding. Do not assume the Inn accommodates all your guests.
