01 · The arrival

The water appears slowly.

Guests leave the garden path and follow the tree line until the river opens in front of them. The reveal is held until the final few steps.

02 · The processional

A long approach
with somewhere to land.

The aisle follows the natural line of the property. From every seat, the willow and water frame the vows without competing with them.

03 · The photographs

Stay after everyone walks away.

Water, meadow, bridge and willow shade give a photographer an entire portrait sequence without moving the couple off property.

The weather plan

Rain changes the setting.
It does not shrink the ceremony.

The historic barn becomes the indoor ceremony setting. The layout keeps every guest comfortable, the processional intact and the celebration in motion.

Accessibility

Every guest should be part of the moment.

Riverside has a step-free approach. On event days, a golf cart and driver are available for guests who would benefit from assistance between the parking area, ceremony and barn.
