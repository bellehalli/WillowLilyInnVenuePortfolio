Cookie & storage policy

Cookies, local storage
& your choices.

Effective date: September 16, 2026.

What this site uses

Willow Lily primarily uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage to preserve demonstration choices such as wedding preferences, date recovery, tour details, proposal status, payment status and CRM demo settings. These technologies are similar to cookies in that they store information in your browser, but they are not traditional HTTP cookies.

Strictly necessary storage

Strictly necessary storage supports core functions such as maintaining the current demonstration state, preserving accessibility preferences, and keeping the requested workflow functioning. This category is not disabled by the consent controls because the demonstration may not function correctly without it.

Optional analytics storage

The current portfolio does not intentionally enable third-party behavioral advertising or optional analytics cookies by default. The consent controls store your preference so future non-essential analytics or measurement tools can be conditioned on that choice before they are activated.

Third-party services

Vercel, Resend and Stripe may use their own technical storage or process technical information when you interact with their services. Their use of cookies or similar technologies is governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose Accept all or Reject all from the site’s consent banner. Reject all records a necessary-only preference for non-essential tracking. You can change your choice later from the Cookie Settings control in the footer.

Clearing stored data

You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Doing so may reset your demonstration wedding, tour, proposal, payment and CRM state.

This policy describes the current portfolio demonstration. A production client should update it to match the exact cookies, analytics tools, advertising tools, consent platform and jurisdictions that actually apply.
