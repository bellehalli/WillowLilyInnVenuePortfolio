Ceremony 01

The Woods

Green, intimate and naturally framed. This is the ceremony setting for couples who want the vows to feel tucked into the landscape rather than staged in front of a building.

Included: rustic benches and arbor. The barn is available as the indoor weather plan.

Ceremony 02

Riverside Dock

Water opens the view behind the ceremony and gives the day a softer, reflective backdrop. It is one of the estate’s strongest photographic settings and a natural choice for couples drawn to the willow and waterfront.

Ceremony 03

The Courtyard

Closer to the estate’s architecture and gardens, the Courtyard feels classic and social. It is an easy setting to personalize with florals while still letting the property do most of the decorating.

Ceremony 04

Covered Bridge

The most distinctly countryside setting on the property. The bridge creates a built-in entrance, a strong aisle moment and a visual that feels inseparable from Willow Lily.
