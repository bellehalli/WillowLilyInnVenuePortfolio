Before your tour

The last few questions.

How many people can the estate host?

Up to 160 people total, including vendors.

Can pets be part of the wedding?

Yes, pets may participate in the ceremony and reception. Inn restrictions apply. A horse may also be accommodated with one large corral.

Are bathrooms indoors?

Yes.

Is the property accessible?

Yes. A golf cart and driver can also help guests who need transportation to the ceremony site.

Are there décor restrictions?

Rice, confetti, glitter, large fireworks and silly string are not permitted. Sparklers may be used away from the barn.

What should we actually decide on the tour?

Your preferred ceremony setting, how you want to use the Inn, which rental structure fits the wedding, and whether the property feels right for your guest experience. Dates, vendors and final logistics can be narrowed from there.
