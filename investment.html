Money & policies

Before you ask.

How do we reserve a date?

A 50% reservation payment secures the wedding date. The remaining venue balance is due six months before the wedding.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes. A $500 refundable security deposit is due 30 days before the event.

Can we choose our own caterer?

Yes. Outside caterers and vendors are welcome. There is not a full commercial prep kitchen, so caterers should arrive prepared for truck-to-table service. A support room provides a sink, refrigerator and ice chest.

Can we serve alcohol?

Yes. Event liability insurance is required for Friday through Sunday, with the appropriate venue and township entities listed as additional insured.

What time does music end?

Music ends at 11:30 PM. Friday and Saturday events conclude by midnight. Sunday concludes at 11 PM.

What happens if it rains?

The barn can become the ceremony location, with guests seated at their dinner tables.

Can we bring a tent?

Yes, up to 40 × 60 feet. A tent does not increase the 160-person maximum occupancy.
