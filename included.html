Know what
you are choosing.

Venue rental supports the celebration. Your catering, photography, flowers and other vendor contracts remain separate.

Ceremony

Four ceremony options; rustic benches and arbor. The barn is the indoor backup, with guests seated at dinner tables.

Reception

Core round and rectangular tables, x-back chairs for indoor use, linens, napkins, rustic bar, chandeliers and twinkle lights.

Guest comfort

Indoor bathrooms, heating and cooling units, parking assistance and golf-cart support in this venue concept. Confirm the actual access route during the tour.

Outside

Bonfire pits, firewood, attendants and lawn games. An optional tent is separate and does not increase the 160-person estate limit.

The Inn

Sunday: 1 PM–11 PM daytime access. One Day: 9 AM–midnight daytime access. Full Weekend: Friday and Saturday overnight stays, sleeping six, with Sunday 11 AM checkout.

Before signing

Agree on exact furniture quantities, staffing, vendor access, event end times, insurance, payment terms and exclusions in the real venue contract. This demonstration is not a booking offer.
