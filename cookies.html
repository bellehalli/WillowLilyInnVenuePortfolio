Cookie & storage policy

Cookies, local storage
& your choices.

Effective date: September 16, 2026.

What this site uses

Willow Lily uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage to preserve demonstration choices such as wedding preferences, date recovery, tour details, proposal status, payment status and CRM demo settings. These technologies are similar to cookies in that they store information in your browser, but they are not traditional HTTP cookies.

Strictly necessary storage

Strictly necessary storage supports core functions such as maintaining the requested demonstration state, remembering your consent choice, and keeping the workflow functioning. This category remains available when you choose Reject all.

Optional interaction analytics

If you choose Accept all, Willow Lily may keep a local demonstration interaction history in your browser, such as pages viewed, builder actions, availability searches and other demo journey events. This information is used only to demonstrate the venue-intelligence experience on this device. It is not a third-party advertising profile.

If you choose Reject all, optional interaction-history recording is disabled and previously stored optional demo interaction history is cleared.

Third-party services

Vercel, Resend and Stripe may use their own technical storage or process technical information when you interact with their services. Their use of cookies or similar technologies is governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can choose Accept all or Reject all from the site’s consent banner. Reject all keeps only strictly necessary browser storage. You can change your choice later from the Cookie Settings control in the footer.

Clearing stored data

You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Doing so may reset your demonstration wedding, tour, proposal, payment, consent and CRM state.

This policy describes the current portfolio demonstration. A production client should update it to match the exact cookies, analytics tools, advertising tools, consent platform and jurisdictions that actually apply.
