Weddings at Willow Lily

This is the day.

How it unfolds

A wedding that moves
without leaving.

The advantage of an estate is continuity. Your wedding party gets ready here. Your guests arrive here. You choose a ceremony setting here. Dinner, dancing, firelight and overnight stays can all happen within the same world.

9:08 AM

Wedding morning

The Inn is already part of the day. Dresses come out, music is on, champagne gets poured and the people closest to you have somewhere to settle instead of hovering in a venue hallway.

3:42 PM

The walk to the ceremony

Choose Woods, Riverside, Courtyard or Covered Bridge. Rustic benches and an arbor are included, with the barn as the indoor plan if Michigan weather decides otherwise.

5:18 PM

Just married

Now the property opens back up. Portraits, cocktails, gardens and the transition into the barn happen without loading anyone into a shuttle.

7:06 PM

Dinner under the lights

X-back chairs, tables, linens, napkins, chandeliers and twinkle lights are already part of the rental. Your caterer and creative team build on top of those bones.

9:51 PM

Nobody is sitting down anymore

The barn stops being a dinner room and becomes the party. Music runs until 11:30 PM on Friday and Saturday, with the event concluding at midnight.

11:24 PM

Meet us by the fire

Bonfire pits, firewood and attendants are included. It is the last exhale before the night ends, or before Full Weekend guests walk back to the Inn.
