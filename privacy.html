Willow Lily Inn & Estate is a fictional wedding-venue demonstration created by A. Halliwell Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this demonstration, how it is used, and which third-party services help operate the experience.

Who operates this website

This portfolio demonstration is operated by A. Halliwell Studio. Willow Lily Inn & Estate is not a real venue and does not accept real bookings. Privacy questions or requests may be submitted through the website’s contact channel.

Information you provide

If you use the private-tour demonstration, you may enter a first name, partner name, email address, phone number, preferred wedding details, tour date and time, and an optional message. The site is designed for demonstration use, so you should use fictional contact details when testing the workflow.

If you use the wedding builder or availability experience, you may also choose a guest count, ceremony setting, package, season, evening preferences, and preferred wedding date.

Information stored in your browser

The website uses browser storage so your demonstration choices can follow you through the experience. Depending on the feature, this may include wedding preferences, selected or recovered dates, tour details, proposal status, test-payment status, local CRM notes, and demonstration interaction history.

Some information is stored in localStorage, which may remain on your device until you clear browser or site data. Other information is stored in sessionStorage, which is generally associated with the current browser session but may be restored by some browsers.

Tour-request email demonstration

Submitting the tour-request form can send a demonstration venue-notification email through Resend. Information entered into that form may therefore be transmitted to Resend and to the configured A. Halliwell Studio demonstration inbox for the purpose of showing how a real venue inquiry workflow could operate. No real venue appointment is created.

Stripe test payments

The proposal workflow can open Stripe Checkout in test mode. Stripe may process information you enter directly on its test Checkout page according to Stripe’s own privacy practices. A. Halliwell Studio does not receive a real payment through this demonstration. The site verifies the resulting Stripe test Checkout Session before displaying a completed test-deposit state.

Do not enter real payment-card information into the demonstration. Use Stripe’s published test-payment details only.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, request time, browser or device information, requested URLs, and security or diagnostic logs. Those records are handled under Vercel’s own policies.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This portfolio does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising. The demonstration primarily relies on browser local and session storage for continuity. If analytics, advertising technology, or additional third-party tracking is added in the future, this policy should be updated before or when that collection begins.

How information is used

Information collected through this demonstration is used to operate the requested demo feature, preserve your progress, demonstrate venue-sales automation, troubleshoot and secure the website, and show how a production wedding-venue system could connect inquiry, availability, CRM, proposal, payment and planning workflows.

How information is shared

A. Halliwell Studio does not sell personal information collected through this portfolio demonstration. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate a feature, including Vercel for hosting, Resend for demonstration email delivery, and Stripe for test Checkout. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users, or the operator.

Retention and deletion

Browser-stored demonstration information remains until it is overwritten, the browser session ends, or you clear browser/site data, depending on the storage type. Email-delivery and hosting providers may retain technical or message records according to their own retention practices. A. Halliwell Studio aims to keep only information reasonably necessary for the demonstration and related security or troubleshooting.

Your privacy choices and rights

You can clear local demonstration data through your browser settings and, where available, the demo’s own reset controls. Depending on where you live, applicable law may also give you rights concerning access, correction, deletion, or other treatment of personal information. You may contact A. Halliwell Studio through the site to make a privacy request.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the demonstration, contact A. Halliwell Studio so the issue can be reviewed.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to operate the demonstration, including HTTPS transport and server-side handling of secret API credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, and users should not submit sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the demonstration.

Third-party services

Vercel, Resend and Stripe operate independently and maintain their own privacy and security practices. When you interact with a third-party service, that provider’s terms and privacy policy may also apply.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the demonstration changes, when new providers or data practices are added, or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact A. Halliwell Studio through the website’s contact channel.

This policy describes the current Willow Lily portfolio demonstration and is not a substitute for legal advice for a production client website. A production venue should have its final privacy notice reviewed against its actual data practices, service providers, jurisdiction, and customer base.
