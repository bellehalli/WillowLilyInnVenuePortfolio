10 answers

How many people can attend?

Capacity

The concept estate limit is 160 people including vendors. Estimate your guest list and vendor team together; 160 invited guests would exceed that limit once staff and vendors arrive.

What changes between the experiences?

Packages

Sunday includes estate and daytime Inn access from 1 PM to 11 PM. One Day includes Friday or Saturday access from 9 AM to midnight. Full Weekend includes Friday 11 AM through Sunday 11 AM, plus two Inn nights.

What is included in the venue investment?

Investment

Ceremony settings, barn reception space, core tables and chairs, linens, lighting and the amenities shown on What’s Included. Food, drink, photography, flowers and other vendor services are separate unless explicitly agreed. This is a venue investment, not a total wedding budget.

What happens if it rains?

Weather

The barn becomes the indoor ceremony setting, with guests seated at their dinner tables. Plan the processional, photography and ceremony-to-dinner transition at the tour.

How do we plan for mobility needs?

Access

Tell us which guests need step-free routes, nearby seating or assisted travel. Golf-cart assistance is part of the concept. Route surfaces and clearances must be checked in person; this demo does not certify an actual property.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Catering

The concept welcomes outside vendors. Plan truck-to-table catering: the support room is not a commercial prep kitchen. Confirm power, safe holding, delivery access, cleanup and staffing with your caterer.

Where should our guests stay?

Travel

The Inn sleeps six and is not accommodation for the whole guest list. Plan nearby hotel rooms and a shuttle for other guests. This fictional venue has no real hotel block or street address; a client launch would add verified options.

How should we sequence the day?

Timing

Start with your ceremony and light preference, then allow time for portraits, cocktail hour and dinner. Confirm vendor arrival windows, weather decisions, music end and cleanup responsibilities during the tour.

What needs confirmation before a contract?

Policies

Reservation payment, cancellation terms, refundable deposit, event insurance, alcohol service, pets, decorations, cleanup fees and vendor responsibilities. This demo does not publish a real venue contract or collect payments.

What happens to this demo request?

Privacy

Wedding preferences are saved on this device. Contact details and lead snapshots stay in this tab’s session and are never sent to a venue. Use fictional details and clear the demo session when finished.
